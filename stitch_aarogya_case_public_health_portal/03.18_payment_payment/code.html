Frequently Asked Questions on Patient Fees

Why is my consultation free?

Under Government of India National Health Directives, all basic OPD consultations and generic medicines distributed in civil and district dispensaries are provided at zero direct citizen fee.

What if I am asked to pay at the counter?

Show your digital Aarogya Case booking receipt with QR token. If an official auxiliary fee applies for specific advanced imaging, an institutional receipt will be generated on portal records.
